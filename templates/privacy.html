Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly (the "Service") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly when you create an account, such as your email address and a securely hashed password. We also collect the expense data you choose to record — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions — so that we can provide the Service to you. We do not ask for or store payment card numbers or bank account credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve the Service: to authenticate you, to store and display your expenses, and to generate the summaries and category breakdowns that help you understand your spending. We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. If you delete your account, the associated expense data is removed from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of third-party services for hosting and for delivering fonts and similar assets. These providers process data only as needed to support the Service and are not permitted to use your personal information for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with third parties for marketing.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at privacy@spendly.app. We will do our best to respond promptly to any concerns.
